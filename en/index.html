PARENT FAQ

Before your child starts the journey

Clear answers to the questions parents usually ask before enrollment.

Does my child need previous programming experience?

No. Previous programming experience is not required, and Level 1 is the natural starting point for most students.

Does the student need a laptop or computer?

Yes. Students need a laptop or computer because they build and practice during the session.

Is instruction in Arabic or English?

Instruction is mainly in Arabic, while technical terms, tools and learning materials are presented in English.

Are there tasks between sessions?

Yes. A short Weekly Mission follows each session to reinforce the skill, and every Level ends with a final project.

How can I follow my child’s progress during Level 1?

Parents receive Progress Reports after Sessions 4 and 8. In Session 12, the student presents the final project in a Parent Showcase and explains what they built, tested and debugged.

Are sessions live and recorded?

Yes. Sessions are delivered live through Google Meet, recorded, and shared with the parent for later review.

What is the difference between Group and 1-on-1?

Group Classes include no more than 4 students and provide structured peer interaction, while 1-on-1 offers fully individual attention and greater flexibility in learning pace.
